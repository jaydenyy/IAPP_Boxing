Welcome

Welcome to Smash Club

Smash Club is the official boxing club of Asia Pacific University, welcoming students of every experience level. Whether you're stepping into the ring for the first time or preparing for competition, our club provides professional coaching, structured training sessions and a supportive community that helps members develop confidence, discipline and physical fitness.

Explore our training programmes, upcoming events, competitions and membership opportunities. Take the first step towards becoming a stronger athlete and a stronger version of yourself.

Club Events

Our Events

Smash Club organises a variety of exciting events throughout the year. These activities help members improve their boxing skills, stay active, build friendships and enjoy a positive club experience.

Smash Club hosts workshops, friendly sparring sessions, fitness challenges and club gatherings throughout the year. Every event is designed to improve members' skills, confidence and teamwork.


Upcoming Activities

Beginner Skills Workshop

Location: APU Level 3 Atrium

Time: 13 Sept (Sunday), 2:00 PM - 5:00 PM

Fees: RM5

Open to: All beginner members and APU students

Learn boxing techniques with experienced coaches and improve your skills through practical training.

Sports Day Showcase

Location: Bukit Jalil Stadium

Time: 18 Sept (Friday), 12:00 PM - 4:00 PM

Fees: Free

Open to: All Smash Club members

Join us for an exciting showcase during national day to display your boxing skills and talents!

Fitness Challenge

Location: APU Level 3 Atrium

Time: 27 Sept (Sunday), 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM

Fees: RM8

Open to: All APU students

Improve endurance, strength and agility through fun activities. Prizes will be awarded to top performers!

Annual Club Gathering

Location: The Savannah Hotel

Time: Oct 16(Friday), 6:00 PM - 10:00 PM

Fees: Free

Open to: All Smash Club members

Annual gathering to build friendships and strengthen teamwork among members. Buffet dinner and entertainment are provided for all members, see you there!

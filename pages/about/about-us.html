About Smash Club

Every student walks in.
Not everyone walks in twice.

Smash Club is the official boxing club of Asia Pacific University — a student-run space where first-timers and competitive fighters train on the same floor, under the same coaches, by the same standard.

Our mission

Boxing as a way to build people, not just fighters

We exist to give APU students a place to get stronger, sharper, and more disciplined than the classroom alone can make them. Boxing is the method — the outcome is students who show up, push through discomfort, and back each other in the ring and out of it.

Every session is built around fundamentals: footwork, defence, and control, before power. We'd rather graduate a technician than a swinger.

Who we train

From first glove to first fight

Most members join with zero boxing background. That's the point. Our beginner track runs year-round, and members who want to compete move into our training and competition squad once they're ready — not before.

No tryouts to join. Just a mouthguard, hand wraps, and a willingness to get hit and get back up.

Our story

From five students in a multipurpose hall to a full club.

Smash Club didn't start with a gym. It started with a booking form and a corner of the sports hall.

2021

Five students, one shared heavy bag

A group of students petitioned APU's student affairs office for gym time and a single heavy bag. Training happened twice a week, whenever the multipurpose hall was free.

2022

Official recognition as a university club

Smash Club was formally registered under APU's student club programme, unlocking dedicated training slots, a small equipment budget, and its first proper coach.

2024

First inter-university competition entry

The club sent its first competitive team to an inter-varsity boxing meet, marking the shift from a training group to a club that competes.

Today

A full training programme, run by students

Smash Club now runs structured beginner, training, and competition tracks, entirely organised and led by its student committee alongside volunteer coaches.

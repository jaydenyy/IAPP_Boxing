In The Gym

The rules

Non-negotiable for every session, every fighter, no exceptions.

01

Gear up, every time

Hand wraps, gloves, and a mouthguard are mandatory for any bag work or sparring. No wraps, no gloves, no exceptions.

02

Sparring needs a coach present

No sparring takes place without a qualified coach supervising the session and setting the intensity level.

03

Match your partner's level

Control your power. Sparring is about technique and improvement, not proving a point against a less experienced partner.

04

Respect the space and the people in it

Clean up after yourself, respect coaches and teammates, and leave attitude at the door. Smash Club is a discipline-first environment.

05

Report injuries immediately

Stop training and tell a coach right away if you or your partner is hurt. Never push through a head injury.

Discipline first

Show up on time, wrap your hands properly, listen before you swing. The habits matter more than the punches.

Respect the floor

Seniors spar with beginners. Coaches correct everyone the same way. Nobody outgrows the fundamentals.

Train for life, not just for fights

Most members never compete, and that's fine. Fitness, confidence, and community are wins too.

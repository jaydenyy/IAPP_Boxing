Our Coaches

The people behind every session

Four coaches, four backgrounds, one standard: technique first, ego never.

Head Coach

Jayden Yeap

A former state-level amateur boxer with over 12 years in the ring, Jayden has coached Smash Club since its official recognition in 2022. He holds a national boxing coaching certification and specialises in footwork and defensive fundamentals for beginners moving into competition.

Assistant Coach

Dexter Yip Zhi Qian

Dexter competed on the national men's amateur circuit before transitioning to coaching. He leads Smash Club's beginner track, focused on building confidence, correct form, and injury-safe habits for members with zero prior boxing background.

Strength & Conditioning Coach

Chan Jing Sheng

A certified strength and conditioning trainer, Chan designs the club's off-ring programming — mobility, endurance, and power work that keeps fighters durable across a full competition season without burning out.

Competition Coach

Jeffrey Lee Jia Ye

Jeffrey has cornered fighters at every inter-varsity meet the club has entered since 2024. A former club-level competitor himself, he now handles fight-camp preparation, sparring strategy, and fighter readiness heading into competition.

Our Committee

The team running the club

Behind every session and event is a committee keeping things organised, on schedule, and on budget.

President

Amirul Hakim bin Rosli

Amirul has led Smash Club since 2023, overseeing club direction, coach coordination, and university liaison. He balances the club's competitive ambitions with keeping training accessible to complete beginners.

Vice President

Tan Wei Ling

Wei Ling supports the president across day-to-day operations and steps in to run committee meetings when needed. She also leads the club's outreach efforts during APU's orientation week each semester.

Secretary

Jackson Li

Jackson keeps the club's records straight — meeting minutes, membership lists, and event documentation. He's usually the first point of contact for members asking about schedules or paperwork.

Treasurer

Marcus Lee Kai Xuan

Marcus manages membership fees, equipment budgets, and event expenses. He works closely with the coaching team to make sure gear replacements and competition costs are planned for well in advance.

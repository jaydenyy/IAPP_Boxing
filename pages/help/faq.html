FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about training, membership, and competing with Smash Club.

Membership

How do I join Smash Club?

Head over to the Join Us page under the Membership menu, fill in the registration form, and pay the semester membership fee. New members are welcome to attend a trial session before signing up.

Do I need boxing experience to join?

Not at all. Smash Club welcomes complete beginners as well as experienced boxers. Our coaches group members by skill level so everyone trains at a pace that suits them.

How much does membership cost?

Membership fees vary by semester and are listed on the Join Us page. Fees cover training sessions, use of club equipment, and entry into club events.

Training

When and where are training sessions held?

Training schedules, venues, and session times are posted on the Training page under Activities. Sessions typically run several evenings a week at the APU sports hall.

What should I bring to my first session?

Wear comfortable sportswear, bring a water bottle and a towel. Hand wraps and gloves can be borrowed from the club for your first few sessions, after which we recommend getting your own.

What equipment do I need to buy eventually?

Basic essentials include hand wraps, boxing gloves, and a mouthguard. Details and recommended suppliers can be found on the Equipment page under Resources.

Competitions & Events

Can beginners take part in competitions?

Competitions are open to members who have completed a minimum training period and been cleared by a coach. Beginners are encouraged to build up skill and confidence through regular training first.

How do I find out about upcoming events?

All upcoming events, sparring nights, and tournaments are announced on the Event page under Activities, and shared on our social media channels linked in the footer.

General

Can I volunteer without being a competing member?

Yes. The club welcomes volunteers to help with event organisation, coaching support, and media coverage. Visit the Volunteer page under Membership to sign up.

How do I contact the club if my question isn't answered here?

You can reach us through the Contact Us page, by email, or by phone. Our details are also listed in the footer of every page.

Still have questions?

Our team is happy to help with anything not covered above.

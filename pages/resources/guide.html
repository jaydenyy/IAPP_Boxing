Basics of Boxing

Boxing Guide

Welcome to our boxing guide! Boxing is a combat sport that improves strength,speed,endurance,and self-confidence.This guide introduces the basic skills every beginner should learn before starting boxing training.

🧍‍♂️ Basic Stance

  • Keep your feet shoulder-width a part.
  • Place your stronger foot behind.
  • Keep your hands up to protect your face.
  • Prothect your chin.
  • Stay balance at all times.

🥊 Basic Punches

  • Jab: A quick straight punch used to measure distance and set up other punches.
  • Cross: A powerful punch thrown with the rear hand, usually following a jab.
  • Hook: A curved punch aimed at the side of the head or body.
  • Uppercut: An upward punch targeting the opponent's chin.

🛡️ Basic Defense

  • Keep your hands up to protect your face and chin.
  • Block incoming punches with your golves or forearms.
  • Stay calm and focused, and be ready to counterattack.

👣 Footwork

  • Keep your feet moving to maintain balance and create angles for attacks.
  • Pratice moving forward and backward, as well as side to side.
  • Good footwork helps you move quickly, maintain balance, and create better attacking opportunities.

🏋️‍♂️ Training Tips

  • Always warm up before training to prevent injuries.
  • Pratice regularly to improve your skills and conditionting.
  • Focus on technique before power.
  • Stay hydrated and maintain a healthy diet.
  • Rest after training to allow your body to recover.

⚠️ Safety Tips

  • Wear hand wraps and gloves.
  • Listen to your coach and follow their instructions.
  • Stop if you fell pain.
  • Always respect your training partners.
